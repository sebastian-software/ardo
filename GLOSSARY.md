# Ardo

Vocabulary for authored content and its publication in Ardo.

## Language

**Post**:
An editorial contribution with a publication date that can be read on its own
and appears in a chronological publication. News and blog entries are posts.
_Avoid_: News item or blog entry as a separate content type

**Post ID**:
The permanent identity of a post across changes to its title, source filename,
or public address.
_Avoid_: URL or filename (when referring to the post's identity)

**Author**:
A person credited with writing a post. A post can have more than one author.
_Avoid_: Publisher (when referring to the writer)

**Author profile**:
Reusable editorial information identifying an author, such as their name,
portrait, biography, and links.

**Publisher**:
A person or organization responsible for making a publication available.
Publishing a post does not by itself mean writing its content.

**Content collection**:
A named set of related content entries with a shared metadata contract.

**Cover image**:
An image chosen to represent a contribution when it is introduced or listed.
It is distinct from images that form part of the contribution's body.

**Source image**:
The authored image from which published image variants are derived.

**Image rendition**:
A version of a source image prepared for a particular display use. Different
renditions can show the same content at different resolutions.
_Avoid_: Source image (when referring to a derived version)

**Core area (A)**:
The author-marked core subject that must remain fully visible. It anchors a
tightly framed composition when no preferred frame is supplied.
_Avoid_: Protected area (when implying preservation alone, without framing intent)

**Preferred frame (B)**:
The author's desired image composition, including how tightly the subject is
framed. It guides the crop while preserving any core area.
_Avoid_: Core area (when referring to a desired composition)

**Framing margin**:
The desired space surrounding the core subject in an automatically composed
image. It expresses preferred breathing room and yields when the available
image content cannot provide it while preserving the core.

**Crop family**:
Image renditions that share the same composition and differ only in
resolution. A different framing belongs to a different crop family.

**Image preset**:
A reusable presentation rule for an image placement, selecting its composition
and responsive display behavior.

**Site page**:
A page whose public identity is independent of documentation releases, such
as the site homepage, a marketing page, or a post.

**Documentation page**:
A page explaining a particular version of an API, feature, or workflow.
_Avoid_: Site page (when referring to version-specific documentation)

**Versioned directory**:
A documentation area whose entire contents belong to a documentation release.
Individual pages share the area's version scope without exceptions.

**Publication date**:
The date and time assigned to a contribution's initial publication. A later
revision does not change its publication date.
_Avoid_: Last updated date (when referring to initial publication)
